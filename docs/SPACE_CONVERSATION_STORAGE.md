# Conversation Spaces, close, and account deletion

This describes the clean-slate model in the Task 4 R1 provisional commits, which are unsealed; its migrations are held (see [../SELF_HOSTING.md](../SELF_HOSTING.md#status)). Archive records (`blue.catbird.labyrinth.batch`, `.mek` and `.attachmentManifest`) live in each member's own repository inside the conversation's Space, on the member's PDS. mls-ds stores no archive ciphertext, batch reference or MEK. The archive design is Labyrinth's [MLS DS backend](../../labyrinth/docs/MLSDS_BACKEND.md).

**Superseded design.** Until 2026-10-31 this document described DS-retained Space batch references (`labyrinth.thread_storage`, `labyrinth.batches`), the `getThreadStorage`, `appendSpaceBatch` and `listSpaceBatches` gateway, and Swan as an external Space authority calling `getSpaceMembership` and `deleteSpaceGeneration` under `LABYRINTH_CONVERSATION_SPACE_AUTHORITY_DID`. Migration `20261031200001_clean_slate_spaces_only.sql` dropped those tables, and none of those routes or settings exists any more. [SPACE_CONVERSATION_STORAGE_VERIFICATION.md](SPACE_CONVERSATION_STORAGE_VERIFICATION.md) is the historical record of that design.

## Conversation Spaces

- **One Space per generation.** Inserting a conversation generation registers `chat.conversation_spaces(conversation_id, generation, space_uri, authority_did)`, with `space_uri` = `at://{authority_did}/space/blue.catbird.labyrinth.conversation/{conversation_id}-{generation}`. The authority is the sequencing DS: this DS for a local conversation, the sequencer for a remote mirror.
- **Authority and host.** The authority's Space host (`com.atproto.space.*`, mounted when `SERVICE_DID` and a P-256 Space-host key are configured) issues credentials, lists writers, and records writer heads. Route contracts are in [../SELF_HOSTING.md](../SELF_HOSTING.md#8-space-authority-host-and-relays-3a).
- **Who may hold a credential.** A participant whose current membership is `active`, homed here or elsewhere. A pending C33 target, a member who left, and a removed member get none. An unknown Space, including one whose conversation was closed and wiped, is `404 SpaceNotFound`. A Space marked deleted, or whose conversation or generation is no longer `active` (for example a `superseded` generation), is `410 SpaceDeleted`.
- **Writes.** A member writes records to their own repository in the Space on their PDS. The writer's PDS reports the write with `notifyWrite`, authenticated by a service JWT whose issuer is the writer. The authority stores the writer's head in `chat.conversation_space_writers`, assigns a monotonic `spaceRev`, and notifies registered syncers after commit.
- **Reads.** A reader presents its proof-of-possession credential with an RFC 9421 signature to the authority (`listRepos`, `getSpace`) and to writer PDSes. A client whose Space authority is another DS gets credentials and reads through its own home's `relaySpaceCredential` and `relaySpaceRead`; the read relay returns `Atproto-Space-Mac` unchanged.
- **Revocation.** Revoking a device revokes the account's live credentials in every active Space. A membership transition that removes or closes a participant period (`commit`, `policy`, `leaveCommit`, zero-leaf `leavePolicy`) revokes that member's. Both send `notifyCredentialRevoked` after commit; closing a conversation sends `notifySpaceDeleted`. These notices are best-effort, over DNS-pinned HTTPS (`M-F04`), so a notified recipient must reject a credential it cached earlier.

## Conversation close and operational wipe

`CatbirdMlsClient::close_conversation` signs the canonical close request and calls `blue.catbird.chat.closeConversation`. A direct participant can close a direct conversation; a group can close only when its final participant is an active administrator.

The sequencer authorizes the close, records its immutable completion and signed terminal entry, and calls `chat.wipe_closed_conversation_operational_rows` in the same transaction. The wipe removes operational conversation history, ciphertext, membership, request deliveries and Space bindings. It releases the remaining retained request charge, including when part of that charge was already reclaimed. Permanent request-attempt receipts, immutable events and operation completions remain as terminal evidence rather than live conversation state.

The sequencer captures the remote homes and Space-deletion targets before the wipe. After the wipe it queues the close to each remote home in the same transaction: a group close carries a `closeAuthority` signed with the sequencer's `#mls-receipt-1` key, and a C33 coordinator queues the close to the peer's transport-only home on the same stream. A queued close does not become due at a home before a delivery to that home that was in flight when the wipe ran has ended. Space-deletion notices go out after commit. A recipient home verifies the authenticated close and applies the same wipe to its mirrored or transport-only conversation. The signed close survives as an attachment keyed by its immutable `conversationClosed` event, not as a conversation row. `getEntries` exposes only that terminal entry to devices in the event's frozen audience, so an offline peer can learn the close without retaining application ciphertext.

Replaying the exact signed close and idempotency key returns the original response bytes without recreating operational rows. A new close against the wiped conversation returns `ConversationNotFound`. The native client retains already decrypted local history, marks its coordinate closed, and refuses further sends.

`ipados_ios_second_device_every_row_branches` covers zero operational residue, peer retrieval of the signed close, rejection of a fresh close, and byte-identical late replay of the original close.

## Account deletion

mls-ds holds no Space batches, so there is nothing to redact or overlay. `blue.catbird.labyrinth.deleteAccountData` runs in one transaction at the account's home DS. Besides deleting the account's push bindings and Space writer rows, revoking its Space credentials, erasing the payloads of its `sendMessage` receipts, and purging its epoch-head chain (7-day `labyrinth.operations` idempotency stays), it does three things:

- **Sequenced entry.** In every active conversation this DS sequences where the account is still an active member, it sequences one `accountDeletedEntry`. That entry is queued to every remote home through the same ordered `deliverGroupEntry` stream as any other entry.
- **Ciphertext prune.** In every conversation this DS sequences, including ones the account left, it prunes the ciphertext of each application message the account sent. Each entry keeps its `seq`, digests and I13 receipt. Every home the message was fanned out to is queued a signed `notifyEntriesPruned`. A conversation whose notice this DS cannot sign keeps its ciphertext, as in the 30-day sweep.
- **Relay to remote sequencers.** A group mirror's or C33 transport-only conversation's seq chain belongs to its remote sequencer, so the home never writes it. Instead it queues a `relayAccountDeletion` for that sequencer, signed with its `#atproto` key. The relay binds the home DID, the sequencer DID, the conversation, the deleted DID and the deletion time. It is retried until delivered; a 404 is final only when the sequencer this DS has on record answers `ConversationNotFound`. A home without its signing key refuses the whole deletion with `503 SignerUnavailable`, and nothing commits.

The sequencer checks the home's signature, its peer policy, and that the caller is the deleted account's recorded participant home (for a C33 pair, the home the account's PDS declaration names). It then applies the deletion exactly as a local one: the entry, fanned out to every home, and the prune with its notices. The entry id derives from the deletion, so a repeated relay sequences nothing more. A wrong home, a non-participant or an altered relay is refused with a 4xx and changes nothing. A sequencer that cannot sign the notices its prune owes refuses the relay with a retryable `SignerUnavailable`.

Clients hide every archived copy of the account's earlier messages once live sync applies its `accountDeletedEntry`. A C33 transport-only home keeps no seq chain of its own, so when the deleted account is homed at its pair's coordinator, the coordinator queues the signed entry to the peer's home as `deliverTransportControlEntry`, in the transaction that sequences it. The queue keeps it behind the pair's earlier deliveries to that home by `seq` and retries it until delivered. The peer's home is resolved from its declaration before that transaction; a peer home that does not resolve refuses the deletion with a retryable `503 PeerHomeUnavailable`, while a peer whose DID or declaration is gone is owed nothing. Before any effect, the home checks that the caller is the pair's recorded coordinator and the entry's sequencer, that the deleted account is its own party's counterparty, the coordinator's `#mls-receipt-1` authority, and that the entry continues its stored seq chain; a gap is refused retryably. It then stores the entry verbatim, so `getEntries` returns it, and prunes the account's held ciphertext. An identical redelivery changes nothing, and another entry at that seq is a definitive `409`.

Every `accountDeletedEntry` is signed evidence. Its required `sequencerAuthority` is the sequencer's low-S ES256 signature with its dedicated `#mls-receipt-1` key (never its `#atproto` key) over `catbird-mls-account-deletion-v1\0` and the length-prefixed `sequencerDid`, `conversationId`, `deletedDid`, `seq`, `entryId` and `receivedAt` of the entry. Local and relayed deletions sign it as they sequence it; a sequencer without that key refuses with `503 SignerUnavailable` before writing anything, so a relaying home retries. A group mirror stores a replicated entry only after verifying its authority against the calling sequencer's resolved `#mls-receipt-1` key, then verbatim. `getConversationState` returns every deletion of the conversation in `accountDeletions`, from the sequencer, a group mirror or a C33 transport home, to any authenticated device of a current member DID: a fresh recovery device with no MLS leaf and no entry entitlement uses it to hide the deleted account's restored history. Non-members get nothing, and the evidence outlives the ciphertext prune.

## Local verification

These tests cover this contract. Task 4 R1 gate results are tracked outside this repository.

- `server/tests/federation_group_and_space_relay.rs` (`--features test-support`): group relay sequencing with an `I13` receipt, the remote-group inventory session rotation, and the `3A` relays (header preservation, SSRF rejection, no new chat rows).
- `server/src/federation/{account_deletion_relay_tests,deletion_evidence_tests,c33_deleted_peer_tests,c33_deleted_acceptance_tests,c33_close_relay_tests,transport_control_entry_tests}.rs`: deletion relays, `sequencerAuthority` evidence, and C33 deletion and close propagation.
- `e2e-tests/tests/ipados_ios_second_device.rs` (`ipados_ios_second_device_every_row_branches`): close residue, peer retrieval of the signed close, fresh-close rejection, and byte-identical replay.
- `e2e-tests/tests/federation_spaces_full_cutover.rs`: two homes, Spaces, relays and push wakes end to end. Commands are in [../server/README.md](../server/README.md#tests).

Production deployment, production migration, and live PDS Spaces interoperability remain separate qualifications.
